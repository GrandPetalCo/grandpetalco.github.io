The Work

Each installation is hand-crafted from premium organza silk, rising seven to eight feet on modular, moss-covered bases — designed to endure.

Not floristry.
Not décor.
Floral architecture.

Grand Petal Co. brings a standard of permanence and craftsmanship to large-scale floral design that the interiors industry has long overlooked. Our installations do not wilt, require no maintenance, and carry no allergy risk — only the sustained presence of something genuinely beautiful.

Every piece is conceived as a permanent feature of its space — a sculptural element with the scale and intention of a commissioned work of art.

The Craft

Made by hand.
Built to last.

Every flower in every Grand Petal Co. installation is shaped by hand from premium organza silk — a material chosen for its translucency, movement, and its ability to hold the quality of light that real petals do.

Installations are mounted on custom modular bases finished in natural moss, designed to integrate with architectural flooring of any kind. Each can be reconfigured, relocated, or expanded as your space evolves.

There are no allergens. No maintenance. No replacements. Only an enduring work of craft, present for every person who moves through your space.

Primary service area: Kitchener–Waterloo–Cambridge, Ontario. Extended service available throughout Ontario — travel and installation fees apply.
