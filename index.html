Our Story

Floral architecture,
crafted by hand

Grand Petal Co. was born from a shared belief that spaces have the power to move people — that the right environment can turn an ordinary event into something guests carry with them long after they leave.

Each installation is handcrafted from premium organza silk, designed to stand 7 to 8 feet tall on modular moss-covered bases. Every petal is shaped by hand. Every arrangement is composed with intention. The result is a living sculpture that anchors a room and becomes the photograph everyone shares.

We are a husband-and-wife team based in Kitchener-Waterloo. We design, craft, deliver, and install every piece ourselves — because details matter, and we refuse to leave them to chance.

Common Questions

Everything you need
to feel confident

Are your flowers real or artificial?

Every flower is handcrafted from premium organza silk — chosen specifically for its lifelike texture, movement, and beauty. The result is indistinguishable from fresh florals at a distance, with the added benefit of zero allergens, zero wilting, and the ability to be used across multiple events.

Do you offer rentals as well as purchase?

Yes — and our rental model is one of our most popular options, especially for corporate clients and venues. You receive the full visual impact of a grand installation without the full cost of ownership. We also offer recurring rental contracts for clients who want a consistent, rotating presence throughout the year.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend reaching out at least 3 to 4 weeks before your event to ensure availability and allow time for any custom design decisions. For larger installations or events during peak seasons, earlier is always better. That said, we will always do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in the Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge region and serve the surrounding areas including Guelph, Brantford, Hamilton, and the Greater Toronto Area. Not sure if we cover your location? Reach out and we will do our best to make it work.

Can you match our event colours or theme?

Absolutely. While each of our named collections carries its own distinct palette, we work closely with every client to ensure the installation feels native to their event. Share your colours, mood board, or theme — and we will guide you toward the right fit, or discuss custom options where appropriate.

Are your installations safe for allergy-sensitive environments?

Yes — completely. Because our flowers are crafted from premium organza silk, they produce zero pollen, zero fragrance, and zero allergens. This makes our installations ideal for healthcare environments, corporate offices, and any venue where guest comfort is a priority.

Do you handle setup and takedown?

Yes — always. We deliver, set up, and install every piece ourselves, and return to take everything down at the agreed time. Your team has nothing to manage before, during, or after. That is part of the service.
