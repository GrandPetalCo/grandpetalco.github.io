The Work

Each installation is hand-crafted from premium materials, rising over seven feet tall on custom architectural bases, built to define a space.

Not floristry.
Not décor.
Floral architecture.

Grand Petal Co. brings a standard of craftsmanship and artistry to large-scale floral design that spaces truly deserve. Our silk floral installations are crafted to the standard of fine art, hypoallergenic and maintenance-free, season after season.

Every piece is designed as a defining feature of its space, carrying the visual weight of a commissioned sculpture with the freedom to evolve as your space does.

Who We Work With

We work with spaces that carry presence. Grand entrance halls, architectural atriums, refined reception areas, curated communal spaces, and the celebrated corridors where presence is everything. If your space welcomes people, we want to make it truly unforgettable.

  • Corporate & Professional Spaces The spaces where your culture lives deserve to reflect it. We create installations that communicate the values, culture, and quiet confidence of the organizations behind them, without saying a word.
  • Hospitality, Retail & Luxury Destinations Every great destination has something that makes it feel unlike anywhere else. We bring presence to hotels, restaurants, private clubs, wellness destinations, boutiques, showrooms, and galleries — installations that draw people in, slow them down, and make every visit feel considered.
  • Healthcare & Community Spaces The spaces where people spend their most vulnerable moments deserve the most thoughtful beauty. We design for long-term care communities, hospitals, and wellness centres with the understanding that what surrounds people every day matters deeply, and deserves to be genuinely beautiful and hypoallergenic too.
  • Landmark & Institutional Spaces Some spaces carry the weight of many. Universities, museums, airports, arenas, and landmark buildings carry a responsibility to their visitors that goes beyond function. We help them meet that responsibility with installations as worthy of the spaces they are made for.
  • Private Occasions Weddings, milestone birthdays, intimate gatherings, family celebrations. The moments that deserve to be remembered deserve to look the part. We work with private clients to create something genuinely extraordinary for the occasions that matter most.

The Craft

Made by hand.
Crafted with intention.

Grand Petal Co. was built on the belief that exceptional spaces deserve exceptional craft. Every bloom is shaped individually from premium materials, a material chosen for its luminous quality, its natural movement, and its ability to hold light the way real petals do. The result is an arrangement that arrives ready to elevate any space it enters, available to purchase or rent. No allergens. No maintenance. Only a work of craft, present for every person who moves through your space, and worthy of every glance it receives.
