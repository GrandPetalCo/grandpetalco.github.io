The Work

Each installation is hand-crafted from premium organza silk, rising over seven feet tall on custom architectural bases, built to define a space.

Not floristry.
Not décor.
Floral architecture.

Grand Petal Co. brings a standard of craftsmanship and artistry to large-scale floral design that commercial spaces truly deserve. Our silk floral installations are crafted to the standard of fine art, hypoallergenic and maintenance-free, season after season.

Every piece is designed as a defining feature of its space, carrying the visual weight of a commissioned sculpture with the freedom to evolve as your space does.

Who We Work With

We work with spaces that carry presence. Grand entrance halls, architectural atriums, refined reception areas, curated communal spaces, and the celebrated corridors where presence is everything. If your space welcomes people, we want to make it truly unforgettable.

  • Corporate & Professional Spaces The spaces where your culture lives deserve to reflect it. We create installations that communicate the values, culture, and quiet confidence of the organizations behind them, without saying a word.
  • Hospitality & Luxury Destinations Every great destination has something that makes it feel unlike anywhere else. We bring elegance and beauty to hotels, restaurants, private clubs, and wellness destinations that elevates every moment spent within them.
  • Retail & Experiential Spaces The most memorable spaces are the ones that feel considered in every detail. We work with boutiques, showrooms, fashion houses, and galleries to create installations that draw people in, slow them down, and make them want to stay a little longer.
  • Healthcare & Community Spaces The spaces where people spend their most vulnerable moments deserve the most thoughtful beauty. We design for long-term care communities, hospitals, and wellness centres with the understanding that what surrounds people every day matters deeply, and deserves to be genuinely beautiful and hypoallergenic too.
  • Landmark & Institutional Spaces Some spaces carry the weight of many. Universities, museums, airports, arenas, and landmark buildings carry a responsibility to their visitors that goes beyond function. We help them meet that responsibility with installations as worthy of the spaces they are made for.

The Craft

Made by hand.
Crafted with intention.

Grand Petal Co. was built on the belief that exceptional spaces deserve exceptional craft. Every bloom is shaped individually from premium organza silk, a material chosen for its luminous quality, its natural movement, and its ability to hold light the way real petals do. The result is an arrangement that arrives ready to elevate any space it enters, available to purchase or rent. No allergens. No maintenance. Only a work of craft, present for every person who moves through your space, and worthy of every glance it receives.

Primary service area: Kitchener–Waterloo–Cambridge, Ontario. Extended service available throughout Ontario — travel and installation fees apply.
